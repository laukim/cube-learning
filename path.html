Positions and turns

Every position of the cube is a node. Every face turn is an edge to a neighboring position. The scramble drops you on a nearby node. Solving is a walk back to the solved node. The fewest turns is a shortest path.

Quarter turns and half turns each count as one move. That is the half-turn metric — the same way a cross solution counts U2 as one turn.
